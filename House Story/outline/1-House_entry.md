The patrol car came to a smooth quiet stop, parking at the curb of number 15, 7th avenue. Jude twisted the key, shutting off the engine, the car ticked as the engine's heat dissapaited. The radio let out a soft beep as he depressed the rubber button located on the side of the device.

"Hey Cheryl, I've just arrived on site, Im goint o have a walk around and check things out, Ill let you know how I go" Jude said into the receiver before stepping out of the car and locking it behind him. The handheld radio came to life with a staticy fuzz "Message received Darl, Be safe" came Cheryl's voice from the device before going silent.

Jude clipped the radio to his belt and brought his torch up to chest height, clicking it on and illuminating the yard of house number 15. The yard was overgrown and appeared to be abandoned. The gravel crunched underneath Judes boots as he walked up the driveway towards the front door, shining his torch through the windows, illuminating the dark empty house within. 

As Jude drew closer to the windows he got a good view of the state of the house, the empty room facing the front yard was empty, the plaster walls stained with water damage and various holes that appeared to have been punched or kicked into the plaster.
