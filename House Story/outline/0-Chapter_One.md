Chapter One.

It was a rainy October night in Sugar City, Idaho. Jude Cole was reclining back in his generically branded desk chair, compliments of his employer IronGate Security, the company logo embossed on a patch situated on the left breast pocket of his navy blue polo. A small television hanging in the corner of the room was playing reruns of Hogan's Heroes, it hung there, out of reach and behind a comically unnecessary metal cage. The volume of the television was set low as per the company's policy, hardcoded in the television as its max output, and in a small town like Sugar City you were lucky to receive any call-outs for an entire shift, but policies are policies and they sure do love enforcing them at IronGate. So what else were you to do? Jude was glad that there was a tv at all, these night shifts would be unbearable without some kind of distraction.

Jude took his feet off of the desk and stretched out, raising his arms over his head and letting out a comically loud yawn. "Aaaahhhnnn" he groaned mid yawn "eh, I need a fresh cup" he announced standing from his chair, turning towards the small kitchen located at the back of the small building. "You want another cuppa Cheryl?" he called out over his shoulder to this weeks co-worker Cheryl Mather's. Cheryl was responsible for answering the phones and tending the enquiry desk at the security firms small office.

"No thank you Love, if I have another one of your strong cups I'll be in the ladies room half the night!" she called back to him with a playful tone as he disappeared down the hallway. "How's that any different from every other night?" he called back teasingly as he filled the kettle with tap water and placed it on the stove, igniting the burner and placing 2 large teaspoons of Folgers instant coffee grounds.
